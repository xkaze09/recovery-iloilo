body & soulphilosophy

Recovery Iloilo brings together the best of three worlds: the ritual and calm of a spa, the precision of body and recovery work, and the warmth of somewhere made for you.

Our approach is unhurried and intentional. We listen first, then tailor each session — combining proven techniques with the small details that turn a treatment into deep rest.
